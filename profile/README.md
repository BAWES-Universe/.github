## What is BAWES?

BAWES builds and operates shared digital environments where people work, learn, trade, and collaborate in real time.

Instead of isolated apps and pages, we focus on connected spaces, live systems, and practical tools that help people move faster together.

---

## Core Projects

### [bawes.net](https://bawes.net)
The main home of the BAWES vision: shared digital environments for teams, learning, commerce, and community.

### [Universe](https://universe.bawes.net)
A live shared environment made of rooms and worlds, where people can collaborate, study, build, shop, and hang out in real time.

### [StudentHub](https://www.studenthub.co)
A platform for paid part-time jobs, student hiring, and payroll operations, connecting businesses with students and fresh graduates.

### [Plugn](https://plugn.io)
A commerce platform for launching online selling quickly, with payments, warehousing, fulfillment, and delivery integrations built in.

---

## How it fits together

- **BAWES** defines the ecosystem and builds the infrastructure.
- **Universe** is the shared spatial layer for interaction and presence.
- **StudentHub** connects people to work, hiring, and execution.
- **Plugn** powers commerce, fulfillment, and real business operations.

Together, they form a connected stack for real-world coordination inside digital spaces.
